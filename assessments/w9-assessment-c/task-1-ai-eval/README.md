# Task 1: AI Evaluation

**About 20 minutes**

> Use AI to produce a summary of the dataset. Compare the AI output to the baseline using at least two criteria (e.g., accuracy, completeness, bias, reliability). Decide: accept / edit / reject, and explain why.

## Steps

1. Open `data/dataset.csv` and look at it for a minute.
2. Fill in `prompt-scaffold.md` and run it in your AI tool.
3. Paste the AI's answer, unchanged, into `ai/ai_output.md`.
4. Read `baseline.md` and compare it to the AI output, row by row.
5. Fill in `ai/evaluation_log.md`: at least 2 criteria with evidence, at least 1 gap or risk, and your decision.

## What's in here

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `data/dataset.csv` | Weekly attendance for four Milwaukee community programs (fictional, no PII) |
| `baseline.md` | A human-written summary to compare against |
| `prompt-scaffold.md` | Fill-in prompt template |
| `ai/ai_output.md` | **You paste** the AI output here |
| `ai/evaluation_log.md` | **You fill in** your comparison and decision |
