# Baseline Summary (human-written)

**Dataset:** `data/dataset.csv` · Fall 2026 weekly attendance for four free community tech programs at Milwaukee sites, weeks of Sept 8 – Oct 13, 2026.
**Provenance:** Fictional training data created by i.c.stars Tech Foundations staff, modeled on a weekly sign-in sheet export. Counts only; no personal information.

---

The file has 25 rows, but one row is a duplicate: Adult Digital Skills for the week of Sept 22 appears twice. That leaves **24 program-weeks** (4 programs × 6 weeks).

Two of those weeks have no usable count. Senior Tech Help recorded 0 on Sept 29 because the site was closed for a power outage, and Homework Help on Oct 6 is blank because the sign-in sheet was never returned. Neither one means "nobody came," so both are left out of the averages.

Across the remaining **22 sessions**, **398 people attended out of 489 registered seats (81.4%)**. Teen Coding Club had the highest rate (87.5%) and Adult Digital Skills the lowest (70.8%), with Adult Digital Skills slipping from 15–16 attendees to 12 by Oct 13.

One count needs checking: Teen Coding Club shows 31 attendees on Oct 13 against 24 registered. Without that row the overall rate is 78.9%. Sherman Park Rec Center is also spelled "Rec Ctr" on Sept 22, so it can look like a fifth site.
