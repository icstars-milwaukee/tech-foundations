# Decisions

Copy this to `docs/DECISIONS.md` in your portfolio repo. One section per real decision. Interviewers love asking "why did you do it that way?" This is your answer, written down.

Good decisions to document: a database design choice, a library you picked (or didn't), how you handled errors or bad data, how you used AI and where you didn't, something you'd do differently.

## 1. <Decision in a few words>

- **Context:** What problem or constraint forced a choice?
- **Options:** What did you consider? (at least two)
- **Decision:** What did you choose, and why?
- **Consequences:** What did it make easier? What did it cost?
