# Risk Register

**Team:** 

A risk is something that **might** go wrong. Write it down while it's still cheap to fix.

**How to fill it in**
- **Risk:** "If ___ happens, then ___." Be specific: "If the events API returns 500 during the demo, the RSVP page shows a blank list."
- **Probability:** Low · Medium · High (how likely?)
- **Impact:** Low · Medium · High (how bad if it happens?)
- **Mitigation:** what you'll do **now** to make it less likely or less bad.
- **Owner:** one person who watches this risk.
- **Status:** Open · Mitigated · Happened · Closed
- **Raised by:** `team` or `peer (team name)`

Run `npm run risks` to sort it by score (Probability × Impact) so you work on the worst ones first.
