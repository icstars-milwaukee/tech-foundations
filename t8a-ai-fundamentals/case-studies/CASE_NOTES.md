**Group members:** 
**Our case (number + name):**  Case 4 Engineers who pasted secret code into a chatbot (2023)

- **What happened (2 sentences):**  Samsungs allowed employees to use ChatGTP which resulted in Samsungs Confidential Information being leaked to an outside service 
- **Which risk (reliability, bias, privacy, or more than one):**  Privacy and security.
- **Who was harmed, and how:** Samsung was put at risk because confidential company information and source code were exposed outside the organization.
- **Where a human could have stopped it:**  Before entering company source code or meeting notes into ChatGPT, employees could have checked company policies or removed sensitive information.
- **The checklist item that would have caught it:** Never share confidential, private, or proprietary information with AI tools.
