**Our case (number + name):** 5 The wrong fact in a launch demo

- **What happened (2 sentences):** 
    - Google had an ad for bard saying the James Webb Space Telescope was the first picture to take an image of a planet outside the solar system, which was WRONG! Astronmers pointed out it was a different telescope. 
- **Which risk (reliability, bias, privacy, or more than one):** 
    - Reliability risk. It gave wrong info.
- **Who was harmed, and how:** 
    - Google's public launch of Bard. They lost $100 billion in stock market value.
- **Where a human could have stopped it:** 
    - A human could have Googled the information to double-check the facts before greenlighting the ad. An astromer could have helped too.
- **The checklist item that would have caught it:** 
    - Every fact, number, name, and date is checked against a real source.
